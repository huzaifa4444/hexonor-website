FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Hexa answers instantly, 24/7, from our own knowledge base.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. Hexonor handles everything: setup, connecting your channels, training the AI on your documents, testing and ongoing maintenance.

How does the AI know about my business?

We train your assistant on your own documents (FAQs, price lists, policies, menus and schedules), so it answers accurately about your business.

Will the AI make up wrong answers?

Our assistants answer only from your documents. If the answer isn't there, they say so and offer to connect the customer with your team instead of guessing.

Can it speak Urdu?

Yes. Growth supports two languages (for example English and Urdu) and Pro supports up to five.

Why is there a monthly maintenance fee?

It covers 24/7 monitoring, bug fixes, keeping your assistants working as WhatsApp, Instagram and AI models change, knowledge-base updates and continuous improvement.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. All packages are month-to-month, and you can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice.

What is your refund policy?

The setup fee is fully refundable within 7 days if work hasn't started. Monthly fees aren't refunded for months already started.

Is my data safe?

Your data is used only to run your own assistants. It's never sold or shared, and it's deleted within 30 days of cancellation.
